Research

Analysis, geometry, complexity

My work uses Fourier-analytic ideas to understand geometric configurations, arithmetic structure, signal recovery, and the complexity of functions and data.

Full publication list Google Scholar Research talks

Harmonic analysis and geometric measure theory

A central question is how analytic information about a measure or function forces geometric structure. This includes Fourier restriction, oscillatory integrals, generalized Radon transforms, and the Falconer distance problem.

On Falconer’s distance set problem in the plane · Quanta overview

Uncertainty, spectral synthesis, and signal recovery

This program studies when partial spectral information determines a signal, how uncertainty principles interact with restriction theory, and how geometric support conditions produce rigidity.

Uncertainty principles on finite abelian groups and sparse signal recovery · Hausdorff Institute lecture series

The Fourier ratio and complexity

The Fourier ratio is a simple spectral parameter that measures effective complexity. Current work connects it to approximation, sampling, recovery, localization, learning, and information-theoretic descriptions of signals.

The Fourier ratio and complexity of signals · A unifying measure for recovery, localization, and learning

Geometric combinatorics, finite fields, and number theory

Many Euclidean configuration problems have finite-field and finite-ring analogues. Fourier analysis, incidence methods, group actions, and arithmetic estimates reveal both common structure and sharp differences between settings.

Group actions and geometric combinatorics in finite fields · The Fuglede conjecture in the finite plane

Learning theory and applied data science

Recent projects connect harmonic analysis and geometric combinatorics with VC dimension, statistical learning, time-series recovery, forecasting, and collaborative applied research.

Analytical Methods in Geodesy, Cryptography and Machine Learning · StemForAll 2026 projects
