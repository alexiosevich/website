Vertical Integration Workshop

April 18-19, 2026

Location: Hylan 1106A



The purpose of this workshop is to share the recent results obtained by the members of the Vertical Integration of Research group at the University of Rochester. The first of these workshops was held on November 16, 2025. This workshop will be structured a bit differently. In addition to graduate student talks, several visiting mathematicians will give longer lectures. The talks will be organized thematically to allow the participants to get a broader perspective on variety of topics.
