Vertical Integration Workshop

April 17-19, 2026

Location: Hylan 1106A



The purpose of this workshop is to share the recent results obtained by the members of the Vertical Integration of Research group at the University of Rochester. The first of these workshops was held on November 16, 2025. This workshop will be structured a bit differently. In addition to graduate student talks, several visiting mathematicians will give longer lectures designed to introduce the participating graduate students and advanced undergrads to the topics related to their current area of research. The talks will be organized thematically to allow the participants to get a broader perspective on variety of topics.

In preparation for the workshop, reading materials will be posted on this website that will allow the participate to gain some background knowledge for the plenary lectures.


Plenary lectures:

Masha Gordina (University of Rochester)

Camil Muscalu (Cornell)

Eyvi Palsson (Virginia Tech)

Steven Senger (Missouri State)

Krystal Taylor (Ohio State)

Alexia Yavicoli (UBC)
