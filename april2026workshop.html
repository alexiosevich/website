Vertical Integration Workshop, April 16-19





We had so much fun when ran this workshop in October, we decided to do it again! This time, we are going to have faculty, postdocs, graduate students and undergraduates listening to each other's talks and interacting throughout. The talks are labeled (F) for faculty, (P) for postdoc, (G) for gradstudent, and (U) for undergrad.
