Past and Current Ph.D. Students

A gathering with several of my Ph.D. students, postdocs, and other friends

This page is dedicated to my past and present Ph.D. students. I have been extraordinarily fortunate to advise so many talented and dedicated people. Their accomplishments reflect their own ability, creativity, and hard work. I am grateful to have had the opportunity to support them during an important stage of their careers.

The list includes students advised at the University of Missouri and the University of Rochester, as well as students co-advised with colleagues at other institutions where indicated. As of November 18, 2026, the roster includes 38 Ph.D. students. Thirty have graduated and eight are current students.
